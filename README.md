Newest test build: version 0.3.68, Android versionCode 94, nine languages, built from commit 6b57dfe.
New in 0.3.68: every test build from now on is signed with the same key, so it installs over the last one and keeps your data. Uninstall the old app ONCE before installing 0.3.68; after that, never again.
New in 0.3.66: the books (Księgi) are backed up to your account in the ANJAR cloud (EU, encrypted) once you sign in, and come back on a new phone; receipts photographed sideways or upside down are read; the sign-in screen only offers what is switched on.
New in 0.3.65: the Talk button no longer switches the microphone off and on (one quiet retry, then "Tap to talk again"); the phone receptionist (Sekretarka) answers the second turn of a call instead of going to voicemail.
New in 0.3.64: the natural voice keeps one voice for the whole reply (no more switching voices or languages after the first sentence).
New in 0.3.63: the owner's own phone is not sent to voicemail after three test calls a day.
New in 0.3.62: Settings > Voice shows the natural voice first; approved social campaigns can be posted through Buffer (once a Buffer key is set on the server).
New in 0.3.61: security fixes (impersonating call names refused, free trials need a Google/Apple/phone sign-in to call out, clear messages for each).
New in 0.3.60: the female natural voice is fast too (Alice, an ElevenLabs default voice, stands in when Sophia is refused).
New in 0.3.59: the natural voice comes from a faster engine (ElevenLabs) where it can, and Sekretarka answers calls in nine languages with "press 1 for the list" and remembers each caller's choice. The natural voice only starts once the 0.3.59 server is live.
New in 0.3.57: the natural voice. Settings > Voice > Natural voice: one switch makes Anjar read replies in a warm, human-sounding
AI voice in all nine languages instead of the phone's built-in voice. Also in this build (0.3.56): radio and music apps by voice
("Anjar, play Capital FM", "play Adele on Spotify", "open YouTube", "stop the radio"; the radio keeps playing with the screen off).
